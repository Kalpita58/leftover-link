Leftover Link is a practical meeting point for food businesses and community organisations. We make it easier to offer surplus with clear details, coordinate collection, and build reliable relationships close to home.

Terms & Conditions

Participation is intended for registered food businesses and community organisations. Partners are responsible for following applicable food safety, handling, and collection requirements. Contact the Leftover Link team for the full terms of participation.

Privacy Policy

We use the information shared through Leftover Link to connect participating restaurants and NGO partners and coordinate food collections. For questions about personal information or this demo, please contact the Leftover Link team.
